# The perfect sunset: La Maddalena's magic needs no filters (or bottles)

There is a moment in the Archipelago when time seems to stand still. It is when the sun, tired of shining, begins its slow descent, diving into crystal-clear waters and setting the granite ablaze in shades of pink, orange, and gold. Watching the sunset from Punta Tegge, Caprera, or a cliff overlooking the sea is an experience that stays in your heart, a ritual every visitor should allow themselves.
Many people love to pair this spectacle with an aperitif: a cold beer, a glass of local wine, a toast with friends. It is a moment of joy and sharing. But for this magic to remain intact for everyone, we need a small pact of respect between us and nature.

## The thrill of beauty, not excess

A good glass can enrich the atmosphere, but it should never become the main character. La Maddalena deserves to be experienced with all senses fully awake. Moderate, mindful drinking lets us truly enjoy the colors, scents, and sound of the shoreline, without alcohol clouding the wonder around us. Let's toast to beauty, but with the clarity needed to appreciate it fully.

## Glass: an unwelcome guest on the cliffs

The real danger, however, lies in what we leave behind. Glass bottles abandoned on beaches or wedged into rocky cracks (such as at Punta Tegge, where the granite seems so inviting) are far more than simple litter:

- **Risk of injury:** Broken glass on a beach or cliff where people walk barefoot is a serious hazard for swimmers, children, and animals.
- **Fire hazard:** Under certain conditions, the lens effect of glass in the sun can trigger small fires in dry Mediterranean scrub.
- **Long-term pollution:** A glass bottle can take up to 4,000 years to fully decompose, shattering into tiny fragments that become almost impossible to remove.
- **Visual damage:** Nothing breaks the spell of a sunset like a carpet of empty bottles at your feet.

## The responsible sunset toast checklist

Here is how we can make sure our toast is a tribute to the beauty of the Archipelago, not a scar on its skin:

1. **Drink in moderation:** Savor alcohol the same way you savor the view, slowly and respectfully.
2. **Take away what you bring:** If you brought a bottle or cans, the rule is simple: take them back with you.
3. **Do not hide waste:** Granite cracks are not bins. Do not leave empty bottles between rocks hoping no one will notice.
4. **Sort waste back home or at the bar:** If you bought alcohol at a supermarket, bring empty bottles to glass-recycling containers once you are back at your accommodation. If you bought it from a beach kiosk, make sure to return the bottle or throw it in the venue's designated bin.
5. **Become a "Sunset Guardian":** If you see an abandoned bottle, pick it up and take it away. A small collective action can make a huge difference.

> "Sunset is nature's gift. Our respect is the thank-you note."

Visiting La Maddalena is a privilege. Leaving the sunset spot exactly as you found it (or even a little better) is the most sincere way to say "thank you" to this wonderful land that gives us unique emotions every evening.

**Do not break the sunset spell, protect the Archipelago.**
