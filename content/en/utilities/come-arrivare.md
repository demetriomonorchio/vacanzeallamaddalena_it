# How to get to La Maddalena

## The Palau ferry ritual

La Maddalena is an island: to reach it, you take the ferry from Palau. The crossing takes about 15 minutes and marks the real boundary between mainland routine and archipelago pace.

Palau is easy to reach:

- **By car from Olbia** (Costa Smeralda airport): about 55 km, around 45 minutes.
- **By car from Alghero**: about 145 km, around 2 hours.
- **By bus**: seasonal ARST and Turmo Travel routes connect major hubs to Palau port.

## Which ferry operator?

Two operators run the Palau-La Maddalena route all year with regular frequency:

- **Delcomar** ([delcomar.it](https://delcomar.it)): the historic operator with very frequent crossings.
- **Maddalena Lines** ([maddalenalines.it](https://www.maddalenalines.it/)): modern vessels and extra departures at peak times.

**Golden rule for August**: book online in advance on official websites. Pre-booking gives you priority boarding and avoids long waits in the heat.

# Choosing your apartment by logistics

Where you stay defines your day-to-day flow. Use this quick map:

### **Madda: village centre life**

- **Best for**: couples and guests who enjoy restaurants, evening walks, and being close to everything.
- **Logistics**: **no car needed**. You can walk to shops, dinner spots, and the ferry area. For beaches, use local buses or rent a scooter.

### **Lena: privacy and quiet**

- **Best for**: guests who want outdoor space and a calmer base.
- **Logistics**: **vehicle recommended**. Slightly farther from the core centre, but closer to coastal spots and quieter areas.

### **Isola: sea view first**

- **Best for**: guests who prioritise views and easy access to sea routes.
- **Logistics**: **vehicle recommended**. Less central, more convenient for independent beach hopping.

# Ways to explore freely

### 1. Scooter: the most practical option

Scooters are often the easiest way to move between the village and Caprera coves, especially in high season.

- **Tip**: in summer, reserve early by phone or website.

### 2. Bicycle and e-bike: slower, scenic rhythm

Ideal if you want a quieter pace through Mediterranean scrub and coastal roads.

- **E-bike** helps on Caprera gradients.
- If you stay at **Isola** or **Lena**, parking makes bike logistics easier.

### 3. Public transport: simple and panoramic

Local routes connect the centre with major beaches and viewpoints. It is the easiest low-stress option for guests staying in central La Maddalena.

## Demetrio's tip

If you want village life and evening strolls, choose **Madda** and move mostly on foot. If your priority is sea view or post-beach quiet, **Isola** and **Lena** are ideal - in that case, rent a scooter and the whole archipelago becomes quick and easy to access.
