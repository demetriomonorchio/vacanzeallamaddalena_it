# Island services

## A practical reassurance

La Maddalena is not a remote island. You will find everything needed for independent stays: stocked supermarkets, rotating duty pharmacies, a working hospital, ATMs, and reliable mobile coverage on the main island.

This guide is for guests staying in apartments who want to know exactly where key services are, with practical expectations rather than generic travel advice.

## Supermarkets

The closest option to the centre is **Dettori** (Via Principe Amedeo 1), a reliable everyday choice for fresh produce and essentials.

A second, smaller supermarket in Via XX Settembre is useful for quick top-ups.

The **morning market** (in season, usually Tuesday to Saturday) is best for fresh fish, fruit, vegetables and local cheeses. Arrive early for best selection.

## Pharmacies

**Farmacia Dr Max** (Piazza Santa Maria Maddalena, 5/B) is a central reference point. Night and Sunday service rotates across island pharmacies; current duty info is displayed at each pharmacy and available online by postcode (**07024**).

## Hospital and emergency care

**Paolo Merlo Hospital** is located in Via Ammiraglio Magnaghi, a short distance from the centre. Emergency care is active year-round, with reinforced staffing in peak season.

For major emergencies, transfer to Olbia hospital is handled through established emergency protocols.

## ATMs and payments

Main ATM points include:

- **Banca di Sassari** (Via Amendola)
- **Poste Italiane** (Piazza Umberto I)
- **Banco di Sardegna** (Via XX Settembre)

Card payments are widely accepted, but carrying some cash is still useful for market stalls, small operators and occasional taxi payments.

## Connectivity: phone and WiFi

4G is generally stable across the island; 5G availability depends on operator and area.

WiFi in the Isola, Madda and Lena apartments is included and suitable for remote work and video calls. In August evenings, mobile networks may slow down due to load; apartment WiFi is often more stable.

## Sailing services

If your stay is sailing-focused, La Maddalena offers a complete chain: sailing schools, day-sail operators, marina infrastructure and practical support services.

**Schools and training**

- **Centro Velico Caprera**
- **Club Nautico La Maddalena**
- **Associazione Acque Libere**

**Day sails and charter-style outings**

- **Veliero Helena**
- **Fil Rouge Sail**
- **Veliero Dolce Vita**
- **Velieri Valentina**
- **JOY Sailing**
- **Sea Passion** (catamaran format)

**Mooring and marina references**

- **Porto Cala Mangiavolpe**
- **Cala Gavetta**
- **Marina di Punta Nera**

In peak season, availability can change quickly. Always confirm by direct contact shortly before departure.
