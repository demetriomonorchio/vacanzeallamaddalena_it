# The National Park of the Archipelago

## 18,000 hectares. 180 km of coastline. One starting point.

The Geomarinee Park of the La Maddalena Archipelago is one of the most extensive and best-preserved marine protected areas in the Mediterranean. It covers around 18,000 hectares of sea and land and encompasses seven major islands — La Maddalena, Caprera, Santo Stefano, Budelli, Santa Maria, Razzoli and Spargi — along with numerous rocks, shoals and seabeds unique in the world.

With 180 km of coastline to explore, our apartments in La Maddalena are the perfect starting point for every expedition into the Park — by boat, on foot, by bicycle, or simply following the day's wind.

## The Pelagos Sanctuary

The Park forms part of the International Sanctuary for Marine Mammals "Pelagos" — an area of 87,500 km² covering the Ligurian Sea, the northern Tyrrhenian Sea and the waters around Corsica. Sperm whales, fin whales, striped dolphins and bottlenose dolphins frequent these waters. Sightings during boat excursions to the outer islands are not uncommon.

The archipelago also forms part of the International Marine Park of the Bocche di Bonifacio, managed jointly with France's Corsican Natural Reserve — a cross-border collaboration that reflects the singular nature of this ecosystem.

## Park zoning

The Park is divided into zones with different rules, designed to protect the most fragile areas while guaranteeing sustainable access to the rest.

Zone A is the most protected: access reserved, no unauthorised human activity. Zone B is semi-protected: navigation permitted, diving regulated, anchoring subject to restrictions. Zone C is the most accessible: recreational boating and sports activities allowed with a standard permit.

Understanding which zone you are in — and respecting it — is not bureaucracy: it is the condition for finding the same transparent water here in twenty years' time.

## Permits and authorisations

The Park authority manages authorisations for access to the marine protected areas. Permits can be obtained online or directly at the office.

Recreational boating (private vessels): one day costs between €2 and €5 depending on zone; seven days between €34 and €760; fifteen days between €58 and €1,440. Tariffs vary by vessel size and navigation zone.

Scuba diving: annual permit €75, day permit €5.

Sport fishing from shore: free for non-residents, issued at the authorisations office up to a limit of 100 permits per calendar month. Under-16s are exempt.
